Trip Komodo Island is a digital platform developed by Garuda Pariwisata to provide information and booking services for tour packages in the Labuan Bajo area and its surroundings. The website is designed to provide an intuitive, engaging, and informative user experience for domestic and international travelers who want to explore exotic tourist destinations such as Komodo Island, Waerebo, and Flores.
